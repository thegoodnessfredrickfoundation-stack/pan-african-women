How it works

From search to payment, in five clear steps.

01

Search talent

Filter by skill, category, country, rate, rating and availability to find the right woman for the job.

02

Post a job

Describe the work, set a fixed or hourly budget and a deadline. Or hire directly from a profile.

03

Review proposals

Compare cover messages, delivery times and portfolios. Shortlist, message and interview.

04

Fund securely

Your payment is held in escrow until milestones are delivered and approved. No raw card data stored.

05

Approve & review

Approve delivery to release the payout, then rate quality, communication and timeliness.

01

Create your profile

Add your title, bio, skills, languages and experience. A shareable link is yours: /@username

02

Build your portfolio

Upload project images, outcomes and the tools you used. Link GitHub, Behance or your site.

03

Publish services

Create fixed-price gigs with delivery time, revisions and optional package levels.

04

Apply & get hired

Browse jobs and submit proposals. Track status from submitted to accepted.

05

Deliver & get paid

Message in-platform, deliver through the workspace and receive a protected payout.

Trust & safety

Built to protect both sides of the deal.

🔐

Secure by design

Hashed passwords, role-based access, validated uploads, rate limiting, secure sessions and HTTPS everywhere.

💳

Escrow payments

Funds are held securely until delivery is approved. No raw card details are ever stored on the platform.

⚖️

Dispute handling

Report a user, job or review. Our admin team moderates, suspends and bans where needed.

🛡️

Anti-fraud controls

Verification badges, spam controls and audit logs on every important admin action.

📜

Clear policies

Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and marketplace rules, aligned with African data-protection laws.

🤝

Real reviews only

Ratings come from completed projects, so reputations reflect genuine delivery.
